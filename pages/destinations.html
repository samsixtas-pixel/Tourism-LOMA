Explore the world's most celebrated national parks and marine sanctuaries with certified local safari guides.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Serengeti National Park

Named 'Siringit' (Endless Plains) by the Maasai, Serengeti hosts over 1.5 million wildebeest and hundreds of thousands of zebras on their annual circular migration. Home to apex predators: lions, leopards, cheetahs, and hyenas.

Highlight: River crossings (Jul-Oct) & Calving season (Jan-Feb).

The World's Largest Intact Caldera

Ngorongoro Crater

A 600-meter deep volcanic caldera housing over 25,000 large mammals within a natural amphitheater. One of the highest wildlife densities in Africa and the best place in Tanzania to spot endangered black rhinos.

Highlight: Year-round Big Five sightings on the crater floor.

The Elephant Sanctuary

Tarangire National Park

Famous for colossal baobab trees that dot the river valleys and massive elephant herds numbering up to 300 individuals during the dry season along the permanent Tarangire River.

Highlight: Giant elephant herds and exceptional bird watching (550+ species).
