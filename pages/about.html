Our Story & Purpose

"Explore More. Live the Journey." We are native Tanzanian adventurers committed to sharing the true spirit of Africa with integrity and warmth.

Our Philosophy

Authentic Connections, Uncompromised Safety

LOMA Adventures was born from a desire to show international travelers the real Tanzania—not through insulated bubbles, but through genuine local connection. Our team consists of native Tanzanian guides, mountain leaders, and safari planners born and raised near the slopes of Kilimanjaro and the plains of Arusha.

We operate fully customized private 4WD safaris, high-altitude mountain expeditions, and tropical Zanzibar retreats with fair porter wages, community-led conservation support, and meticulous attention to traveler comfort.
