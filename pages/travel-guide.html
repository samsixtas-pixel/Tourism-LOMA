Complete Tanzania Travel Guide

Everything you need to know before stepping foot in East Africa: visas, flights, vaccines, safari currency, and tipping etiquette.

Entry Formalities

Visa & Entry Requirements

Passport holders from the USA, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia require a tourist visa to enter Tanzania. You can easily obtain an ordinary single-entry tourist visa ($50 USD for most nationalities, $100 USD for US citizens which grants multiple-entry validity) via the official Tanzania Immigration e-Visa portal online at least 3 weeks before travel, or upon arrival at major international airports (JRO, DAR, ZNZ).

Important Passport Rule: Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your departure date from Tanzania and have at least 2 consecutive blank pages.
Medical & Health

Vaccines & Health Preparation

Malaria: Tanzania is located in a malaria-endemic region. We strongly recommend consulting your travel clinic for prophylactic medications (such as Atovaquone-Proguanil/Malarone or Doxycycline).

Yellow Fever: A Yellow Fever vaccination certificate is mandatory only if you are arriving from or transiting for more than 12 hours through a country with risk of yellow fever transmission (e.g. Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda).

Financial Advice

Currency, Cards & Tipping Etiquette

The local currency is the Tanzanian Shilling (TZS). However, US Dollars (USD) are widely accepted across safari lodges, national parks, and tourist outlets. Important: Due to counterfeiting regulations, US Dollar bills printed before the year 2009 are NOT accepted in Tanzania.

Tipping Guide: Tipping is customary and deeply appreciated by the hard-working local safari and mountain teams:

  • Safari Driver-Guide: $20 - $30 USD per day per vehicle (shared among travelers)
  • Kilimanjaro Chief Guide: $20 - $25 USD per day from the climbing group
  • Lodge Staff Communal Tip Box: $10 - $15 USD per day for all lodge staff
