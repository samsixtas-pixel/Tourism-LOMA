Sustainable Travel Pledge

Protecting Nature, Empowering Communities

Tourism should never exploit the landscapes or people it visits. At LOMA Adventures, we operate with transparent ethics: zero plastic waste in national parks, fair living wages and nutritious hot meals for mountain porters, and direct contributions to rural primary education in Arusha.

Porter Welfare & Ethical Standards

Certified guidelines ensuring 20kg strict weight caps, warm mountain gear, and timely payment.

Wildlife Conservation First

Strict off-road driving avoidance in Serengeti, maintaining respectful distance from maternal dens and river crossings.

International Traveler Center

Key practical information for travelers visiting Tanzania from the USA, Europe, Canada, Australia and worldwide.

Visa & Entry Requirements

Most visitors require a visa, easily obtainable via Tanzania's official e-Visa portal or upon arrival at JRO/DAR/ZNZ airports.

View Visa Guidelines →

Getting Here & Airports

Fly into Kilimanjaro (JRO) for Northern Safaris and Kili, or Julius Nyerere (DAR) and Abeid Amani Karume (ZNZ) for Zanzibar.

Airport Transfer Info →

Health & Vaccinations

Malaria prophylaxis is advised for safari zones. Yellow Fever certificate required if traveling through endemic transit zones.

Medical Checklist →

Money, Currency & Tipping

US Dollars (printed 2009 or newer) and Tanzanian Shillings (TZS) are standard. Cards accepted at major safari lodges.

Tipping Guidelines →
