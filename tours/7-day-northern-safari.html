Tour Overview

This curated 7-day safari covers the absolute highlights of Tanzania's world-famous Northern Circuit. Witness herds of hundreds of elephants in Tarangire, descend 600 meters into the Ngorongoro Crater caldera teeming with black rhinos and lions, and spend three unforgettable days tracking the Great Migration across the endless plains of the Serengeti.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Arusha (The Safari Capital)

Arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO). Meet your private LOMA Adventures safari guide and transfer to your boutique lodge in Arusha. Evening briefing and dinner under the stars.

Day 2

Tarangire National Park (Elephants & Baobabs)

Drive through the scenic Maasai steppe into Tarangire National Park. Known for its massive elephant population and iconic ancient baobab trees. Afternoon game drive along the Tarangire River.

Day 3

Ngorongoro Crater (The Eighth Wonder)

Early morning descent into the volcanic floor of Ngorongoro Crater. Game drive searching for endangered black rhinos, pride lions, cheetahs, and flamingos on Lake Magadi. Picnic lunch by the hippo pool.

Days 4 - 6

Serengeti National Park (Endless Plains & Migration)

Enter the legendary Serengeti through the Naabi Hill gate. Three full days exploring central Seronera valley and migration corridors. Sunrise game drives capturing predators on kopjes. Optional dawn hot air balloon safari.

Day 7

Serengeti to Arusha / Flight to Zanzibar or Home

Morning game drive on the way to the bush airstrip or return scenic drive to Arusha. Connect to Kilimanjaro airport (JRO) for your international departure or flight to Zanzibar.
